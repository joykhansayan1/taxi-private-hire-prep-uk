Privacy Policy

Taxi & Private Hire Prep UK · Last updated 15 July 2026

Taxi & Private Hire Prep UK ("the app", "we") is an independent study aid for the TfL SERU assessment and the topographical (map-reading) skills test. We built the app to collect as little as possible. This policy explains exactly what is and is not collected.

The short version

The app requires no account, no login, and no email. All of your study data — your answers, progress, streak, bookmarks, flashcard schedule, and mock history — is stored only on your device. We never upload it and we never see it.

What we collect

Study data (on-device only). Your progress and history are saved locally on your device so the app works fully offline. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it any time from Settings → Reset Progress.

Purchases. When you subscribe or buy lifetime access, the transaction is handled by Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes your payment; we receive only an anonymous signal that a valid purchase exists so we can unlock the app. We do not receive your name, card number, or Apple ID.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no ad SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track you.

Notifications

If you opt in to a daily study reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No reminder content is sent through any server.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional private hire driver licence and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights (UK GDPR / GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, there is no personal profile for us to export or delete. You control all app data on your device: erase it via Settings → Reset Progress, or by deleting the app. We do not sell or share personal data — we do not have any to sell.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.
